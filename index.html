EMERGING AREAS

Chapter Topics

We invite contributions across six cutting-edge thematic areas shaping the future of sustainable desalination.

01

AI & Machine Learning in Water Systems

Neural networks, deep learning, predictive modeling, and optimization algorithms for desalination process control and performance enhancement.

Deep Learning Optimization Predictive Models
02

Digital Twins & Smart Monitoring

Virtual replicas of desalination plants, real-time IoT-based monitoring, fault detection, and predictive maintenance frameworks.

IoT Sensors Digital Twins Real-time Data
03

Renewable Energy Powered Desalination

Solar, wind, and geothermal integration with RO, MD, and ED systems; hybrid renewable-desalination configurations and grid interactions.

Solar PV Wind Energy Hybrid Systems
04

Circular Economy & Resource Recovery

Brine valorization, mineral extraction, zero liquid discharge (ZLD), and closed-loop water-energy-nutrient systems.

Brine Mining ZLD Circular Design
05

Advanced Membrane & Hybrid Processes

Nanomaterial-enhanced membranes, forward/reverse osmosis, membrane distillation, electrodialysis, and hybrid process intensification.

Nanomembranes FO/RO/MD Hybrid
06

Techno-economic & Sustainability Assessment

Life cycle assessment (LCA), exergy analysis, cost modeling, carbon footprint evaluation, and policy frameworks for sustainable desalination.

LCA Exergy Cost Modeling

SUBMISSION GUIDELINES

Chapter Requirements

All chapters must follow Elsevier's formatting guidelines and meet the following requirements.

Chapter Length

8,000 – 9,000 words (inclusive of references, figures, and tables)

Abstract

300 – 500 words covering objectives, methodology, key findings, and significance

Figures & Tables

8 – 10 high-resolution figures/tables in editable format (vector preferred)

Keywords

5 – 8 relevant keywords for indexing and discoverability

Formatting

MS Word / LaTeX, Elsevier house style, double-spaced, numbered sections

References

APA / numbered style, recent publications (last 10 years), cross-referenced in text

Originality

Original, unpublished work. Plagiarism check via iThenticate (≤15% similarity)

Authorship

Multi-institutional and international collaborations are strongly encouraged
